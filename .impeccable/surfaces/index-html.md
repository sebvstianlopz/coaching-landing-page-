# Surface brief: index.html (landing page)

Scope: the single landing page linked from Jan Medina's Instagram bio. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience and job: people in Puerto Rico, on a phone in Instagram's in-app browser, deciding whether Jan can get them a result. Action: message Jan on WhatsApp. Proof: real client before/after photos, real numbers and quotes (user supplies; samples marked until then).
Direction pinned by the user from four references (Movewell is primary; Marcus Reid, Be Greater and Kropp contribute photo-led presence and overlapping frames). Code-led build: no image generation in this session.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm gallery wall of real bodies and real light. Photographs hang at uneven sizes and overlap like prints; the transformation prints are the ones you can pull apart. Refuses the category default: a dark gym page with red glow, stat cards and icon program tiles.

OWN-WORLD: Cool sea-mist ground, deep El Yunque green as the only accent and as one inset panel, near-black green ink. Hanken Grotesk light at large sizes in sentence case, quiet small text. 16px radius on every photo and panel, pill buttons, no lines, no shadows. Daylight photographs of sea, sky and training carry all colour.

STORY: The visitor meets Jan large and in daylight, understands "strength and endurance, at your pace", pulls apart real before/afters, sees real numbers, learns the four steps from a first WhatsApp message, gets practical answers, and taps "Agenda por WhatsApp" from any screen.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A light headline across the top third at 6–9vw. Below it, Jan's 4:5 portrait off-centre (columns 7–11) with a small square training photo lower in columns 2–4. The supporting line and the pill button "Agenda por WhatsApp" sit in columns 1–5. The nav is split across the top corners, with the action as the one solid pill top-right.

FORM: Taste "uneven-photographs" (dealt 1 of 3; choice made against place-carries-it and split-stage because it carries both Movewell's calm and Kropp's overlap). seed:8777fd93f3b622ac (rolled after the user pinned the direction from their references; the pinned direction stands). Since then, at the user's choice to use each of 4 photos once: one sticky photo beside the method steps, text-only FAQ and footer, the El Yunque photo reused as the closing backdrop. The panel photo uses an arch mask (Old San Juan doorways) from the Taste shaped-portrait blueprint, pending user confirmation.

SIGNATURE: The before/after slider on every transformation (drag, touch, keyboard), plus the hero portrait settling in from a soft scale and slowly crossfading between Jan's portraits when several are provided.
