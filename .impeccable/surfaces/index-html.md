# Surface brief: index.html (landing page)

Scope: the single landing page linked from Jan Medina's Instagram bio. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience and job: people in Puerto Rico, on a phone in Instagram's in-app browser, deciding whether Jan can get them a result. Action: message Jan on WhatsApp. Proof: real client before/after photos, real numbers and quotes (user supplies; samples marked until then).
Direction pinned by the user from four references (Movewell is primary; Marcus Reid, Be Greater and Kropp contribute photo-led presence and overlapping frames). Code-led build: no image generation in this session.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm gallery wall of real bodies and real light. Photographs hang at uneven sizes and overlap like prints; the transformation prints are the ones you can pull apart. Refuses the category default: a dark gym page with red glow, stat cards and icon program tiles.

OWN-WORLD: White page with sky blue (#38bdf8 accents, #0891d1 buttons with white bold text), pale and light sky bands, navy ink. Hanken Grotesk, light-to-regular display in sentence case. 16px radius on photos and panels, pill buttons. Real daylight photos of Jan and clients.

STORY: The visitor meets Jan large and in daylight, understands "strength and endurance, at your pace", pulls apart real before/afters, sees real numbers, learns the four steps from a first WhatsApp message, gets practical answers, and taps "Agenda por WhatsApp" from any screen.

FIRST VIEWPORT (user-supplied reference, recreated in white and sky blue): nav with wordmark, four section links and an outline 'Contáctame' pill; Instagram and WhatsApp icons stacked at the left; a three-line headline 'Planes personalizados para un impacto máximo' with the sky 'Agenda una consulta' pill below; Jan as a background-removed cutout on the right over thin drifting sky wave lines; two stats stacked top-right (from contenido.js); a thin divider, a short intro bottom-left and a scroll indicator. Background white fading to pale sky with a soft radial glow (client's request).

FORM: Taste "uneven-photographs" (dealt 1 of 3; choice made against place-carries-it and split-stage because it carries both Movewell's calm and Kropp's overlap). seed:8777fd93f3b622ac (rolled after the user pinned the direction from their references; the pinned direction stands). Since then, at the user's choice to use each of 4 photos once: one sticky photo beside the method steps, text-only FAQ and footer, the El Yunque photo reused as the closing backdrop. At the user's request the panel photo is shown whole in the standard frame (no arch), and the accent and panel moved from El Yunque green to sky blue.

SIGNATURE: Real client before-and-afters shown as plain collage prints, exactly as Jan posts them (the user asked for plain photos, no slider), plus the hero portrait settling in from a soft scale and slowly crossfading between Jan's portraits when several are provided.
